2. XML Services

XML projects commonly require a clear understanding of both the source information and the target structure. Before processing begins, the team can review sample files, required tags, attributes, naming conventions, data types, namespaces, schemas, and output expectations. This initial review helps establish a repeatable process.

A structured XML workflow can also make exception handling easier. Missing values, invalid structures, unexpected characters, duplicate records, or mapping differences can be identified during validation and reported for review rather than silently passing into the final output.

For organizations exchanging information with multiple systems or partners, documented mappings and transformation rules provide a useful reference for maintaining consistency. Version-controlled templates, sample outputs, validation results, and process notes can support future updates.

XML support can be organized as a standalone file-processing service or as part of a wider data workflow. The final approach depends on the source systems, file volumes, approved interfaces, security requirements, and business rules.
